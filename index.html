    X and Variables

    Xandvar

    Xandvar (X and Variables) is a streetwear brand built around the unknown: the X, the variable, the path you haven't found yet.

    Its world is raw and grainy — black and white, acid-washed and sun-faded pieces, hand-drawn graffiti marks and touches of acid green.

    Lines like “Don't follow me, I'm lost too” and “X marks the variables” carry its message: the brand doesn't show you the way, it lets you walk your own.
